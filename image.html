Image Editing

Instructional Design | Educational Technology | Digital Learning

Welcome to my Image Editing portfolio! This section showcases digital design projects I created as part of my graduate coursework at the University of Florida. Through these projects, I explored both traditional image editing software and artificial intelligence tools.

Using Inkscape and GIMP, I developed skills in vector graphics, image manipulation, layering, typography, and visual composition. I also experimented with Adobe Firefly and Canva AI to explore how generative artificial intelligence can support the creative design process.

The projects featured below include an original tutoring logo and multiple interpretations of a book cover for A Christmas Carol. These experiences allowed me to compare traditional and AI-assisted design methods while developing practical skills in digital media creation.

A+ Tutoring Logo

An original tutoring logo designed using Inkscape, a vector graphics editing program. This project allowed me to explore typography, color selection, object grouping, layering, and visual composition. I combined text and graphic elements to create a cohesive design suitable for an educational brand.

A Christmas Carol: Traditional Book Cover

A book cover redesign for Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, created using GIMP. This project involved combining and manipulating multiple images, adjusting layers, blending visual elements, and incorporating typography. I emphasized the fallen Christmas tree as a symbol of Scrooge's character and transformation throughout the story.

A Christmas Carol: AI-Generated Book Cover

An alternative book cover for Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, developed using Adobe Firefly and Canva AI. This project allowed me to explore generative artificial intelligence as a creative design tool while experimenting with visual composition, imagery, and typography. I evaluated and refined AI-generated results to communicate the themes of the novel and create a cohesive final design.
