Video Production

Instructional Design | Educational Technology | Digital Learning

Welcome to my Video Production portfolio! This section showcases educational videos developed as part of my graduate coursework at the University of Florida.

For this project, I created two versions of Why Leaves Change Color to explore traditional and AI-assisted video production. Both videos explain the science behind autumn foliage while using different production techniques.

Through this experience, I developed skills in video editing, narration, audio production, visual storytelling, and the application of multimedia design principles.

Why Leaves Change Color: Traditional Video

An educational video created using traditional production techniques. I used Clipchamp to arrange visuals, apply transitions, and edit the video. I recorded my own narration and used Audacity for audio editing. This project allowed me to practice storytelling, pacing, and multimedia design without relying on AI-generated narration or video.

Watch Traditional Video

Why Leaves Change Color: AI-Assisted Video

An alternative version of the same educational topic developed using AI-assisted production tools. I used ElevenLabs to generate narration and AI tools to support visual content creation. I then used Clipchamp to refine the timing, transitions, and overall presentation. This project helped me compare traditional and AI-assisted workflows.
