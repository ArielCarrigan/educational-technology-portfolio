Digital Animation

Instructional Design | Educational Technology | Digital Learning

Welcome to my Digital Animation portfolio! This section highlights my experience using generative artificial intelligence to create educational animations that communicate scientific concepts through visual storytelling.

For this project, I created a short animation explaining how a solar eclipse occurs. The animation demonstrates the Moon moving between the Sun and Earth, casting a shadow on Earth's surface. It then transitions to an observer's perspective, showing the Moon completely covering the Sun's bright disk and revealing the solar corona.

How a Solar Eclipse Occurs

I used Google Flow with Veo 3.1 Lite to generate two animated clips using detailed prompts describing the scientific process, movement, colors, and illustration style. I then used Microsoft Clipchamp to trim the clips, add a crossfade transition, and incorporate a timed text overlay with a fade-out effect.

I also used ElevenLabs to generate the narration and adjusted its speed slightly to fit the animation's 15-second time limit.

Watch Solar Eclipse Animation

Project Reflection

This project gave me the opportunity to explore how generative AI can support the development of short, engaging instructional animations. I learned how important it is to provide detailed prompts that clearly describe the intended visuals and movement.

I also gained experience combining AI-generated content with traditional editing techniques. Although AI made it possible to create animated scenes quickly, careful review and editing were still necessary to produce a clear and scientifically accurate educational resource.
