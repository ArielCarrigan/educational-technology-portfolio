Ariel Carrigan

Instructional Design | Educational Technology | Digital Learning

Welcome to my professional e-portfolio! I am an educator with 15 years of experience teaching middle school mathematics in both traditional and virtual learning environments. I am currently pursuing a master's degree at the University of Florida, where I am expanding my knowledge of instructional design, educational technology, and the development of engaging digital learning experiences.

Throughout my teaching career, I have developed a passion for creating meaningful learning opportunities that make complex concepts accessible to diverse learners. My experience integrating technology into instruction has inspired me to explore innovative approaches to learning and digital content development.

This portfolio showcases projects completed during my graduate studies, highlighting my growing skills in multimedia design, interactive learning activities, web development, and educational technology. I look forward to continuing to build upon these skills and exploring opportunities to apply them beyond the traditional classroom.
